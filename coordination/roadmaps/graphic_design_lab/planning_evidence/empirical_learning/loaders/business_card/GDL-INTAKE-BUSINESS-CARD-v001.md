# BUSINESS CARD — manual intake experiment loader v0.1

Status: DRAFT / EXPERIMENTAL / NON-CANONICAL

Goal: guide a non-designer manager from “потрібна візитка” to a usable Creator-ready task.

Collect only material information:
- sides / size / orientation if constrained;
- person/company name and role;
- phone/email/site/socials;
- logo;
- brand colors;
- QR;
- languages;
- style/references;
- finishing constraints if already decided;
- explicit permission to decide missing aesthetic details.

Missing aesthetic preference is not automatically a blocker. Record explicit customer discretion instead of repeatedly asking subjective questions.

End output:
- confirmed content;
- assets;
- unresolved items;
- customer permissions;
- Creator prompt;
- empirical-case notes.
