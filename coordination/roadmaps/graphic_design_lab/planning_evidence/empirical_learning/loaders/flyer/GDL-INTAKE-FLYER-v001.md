# FLYER — manual intake experiment loader v0.1

Status: DRAFT / EXPERIMENTAL / NON-CANONICAL

Material questions:
- purpose;
- target audience;
- format;
- single/double sided;
- mandatory text;
- call to action;
- contacts / QR / address;
- logo;
- photos/illustrations;
- brand identity;
- deadline/event date;
- references;
- explicit creative discretion.

Behavior:
- collect existing information first;
- ask missing questions in small batches;
- give copy-ready customer messages;
- do not invent factual content;
- preserve unresolved items;
- identify risks before Creator prompt generation.

Record:
- question rounds;
- skipped questions;
- information Creator actually needed;
- first-result acceptance;
- requested revisions.
